📌 Project Overview

This project is an Excel-based e-commerce sales analysis created around a hypothetical Hyderabad-based online retailer selling traditional wear, pearl jewellery, attar and fragrances, Hyderabad food products, footwear, and handicrafts.

The objective was to analyze transaction-level sales data, understand business performance, identify the reasons behind a recent sales decline, and present actionable insights through an Excel dashboard.

🎯 Problem Statement

The business has experienced a decline in sales in recent months, but the raw transaction data does not clearly indicate where the decline is coming from.

The analysis focuses on answering key business questions:

How are monthly sales and profit performing over time?
Which product categories generate the highest and lowest sales?
Which products are underperforming?
Are new or returning customers contributing more revenue?
Which sales channels perform best?
Which Hyderabad areas generate the highest and lowest sales?
When did the sales decline begin?
What factors may be contributing to the decline?
What actions could help improve sales performance?
